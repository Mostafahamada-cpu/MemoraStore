Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our invitations and services

The invitation

What is included with the invitation?

Every invitation includes your chosen design, your event details, the venue location with a map link and a live countdown — personalised for you and ready for every phone. Music, gallery, our story, RSVP and animations are optional add-ons with clear prices.

How do I customise my invitation?

After you order, we personalise names, dates, colours, location and any add-ons you selected. You review it before it goes live — no coding needed.

Can I use my own photos?

Yes. After you order, send us your photos on WhatsApp. To show them in a dedicated photo gallery section, add the Gallery add-on.

Can I change the colours?

Yes. Choose a colour palette in the order form, or pick “Custom / Not sure” and tell us the colours you have in mind.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Every invitation is designed mobile-first and works on phones, tablets and desktops. Guests just tap the link — there’s no app to download.

Can my invitation be in Arabic?

Yes. Choose English, Arabic or both in the order form.

Add-ons

Can I add music?

Yes. Add Music and share the link to your song in the order form — it plays on your invitation.

Can I add RSVP?

Yes. With the RSVP add-on, guests confirm their attendance online and you can track the replies.

Can I add a gallery?

Yes. The Gallery add-on adds a photo gallery section for your favourite moments.

Can I add our own story?

Yes. The Our Story add-on adds a dedicated section that tells your story.

Ordering & delivery

Can I request changes?

Yes. You review a preview before your invitation goes live and can ask us to adjust your details. Updates and fixes are included at no extra cost.

How fast will I receive my invitation?

We start as soon as your order is confirmed on WhatsApp and agree the delivery time with you there — it depends on the design and add-ons you choose. You always see a preview before it goes live.

Can I share it on WhatsApp?

Of course. Your invitation is a website link — share it on WhatsApp, Instagram, by text message or anywhere else, and guests open it instantly.

Do I keep access after the event?

Yes. You get lifetime access — your invitation stays online after the event as a keepsake of your day. Updates and fixes are included at no extra cost.

What payment methods do you accept?

We currently accept InstaPay. After payment, send your payment screenshot via WhatsApp and our team will activate your order.
